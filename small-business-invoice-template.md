1. Purpose
The purpose of this template is to provide freelancers, contractors, and small business owners with a clean, simple, professional invoice they can use to bill clients quickly and consistently. The template should be easy to customize, printable, and usable in Google Sheets, Excel, or PDF format.

2. Target Users
Freelancers (designers, editors, developers, marketers)

Small business owners

Contractors and service providers

Anyone who needs a simple, professional invoice

These users value clarity, speed, and professionalism.

3. Core Features
Each invoice template must include:

Business Information

Business name

Address

Email

Phone

Website (optional)

Client Information

Client name

Client company (optional)

Client email

Client address

Invoice Details

Invoice number

Invoice date

Due date

Payment terms (e.g., “Net 30”)

Service Breakdown Table  
Columns:

Description of service

Quantity

Rate

Line total

Totals Section

Subtotal

Tax (optional)

Discounts (optional)

Grand Total

Payment Instructions

Accepted payment methods

Bank info / PayPal / Stripe link

Notes (optional)

Footer

“Thank you for your business” or similar message

4. Design Requirements
Clean, minimal layout

Easy to read

Professional fonts (e.g., Inter, Montserrat, Arial)

Light use of color (optional)

Works in both light mode and print mode

Fits on one page

5. File Formats to Provide
Google Sheets version

Excel version

PDF export

Optional: Canva version

6. Variations to Include
To increase value and allow bundling:

Service‑based invoice (default)

Hourly invoice (with hours × rate)

Product invoice (with SKU, quantity, unit price)

Simple minimalist invoice (ultra‑clean)

Brand‑colored invoice (one accent color)

Export as PDF before sending
